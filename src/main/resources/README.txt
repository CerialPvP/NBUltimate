------------------ NBULTIMATE README ------------------
Hello, and thank you for downloading NBUltimate on your Minecraft server.
This plugin allows you to play music files, such as NBS (Note Block Song), MIDI, MCSP2 (legacy format) and NoteBot.

------------------ DEFAULT SONGS ------------------
The songs in your songs folder are the default songs that come with your NBUltimate installation.
You may use them to test out the plugin, and see if everything works.
